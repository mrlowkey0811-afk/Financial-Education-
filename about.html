The story behind Pocket Ledger.

Why a teen decided the money basics needed a teen explaining them.

JT
The Founder

Hi, I'm Jaiden T. — I'm 14, and I started Pocket Ledger.

Nobody ever sat me down and explained money — budgeting, saving, what a paycheck even means. I learned most of it the slow way, by messing it up first. Pocket Ledger is the resource I wish I'd had: short, honest lessons from someone your own age, not a textbook.

I'm not a financial advisor and I'm not pretending to be one. I'm a teen who got curious about money, started learning, and figured other teens might want to learn alongside me.

The whole idea in one sentence: learn it before you need it.

Most people figure out money by making expensive mistakes as adults. Pocket Ledger exists so teens get a head start — the basics explained plainly, years before they actually matter most.
